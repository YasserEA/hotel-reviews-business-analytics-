# Key Insights

## 1. Overall experience
- Customers are mostly satisfied: 58.8% of reviews are positive and the average rating is 3.76.
- About one review in five (19.9%) is negative, which is 6,333 reviews.

## 2. What goes wrong most often
- **Room and Comfort** and **Cleanliness** account for the largest number of complaints (2,773 and 2,451).
- **Staff and Service** (1,927), **Food and Breakfast** (1,150), and **Maintenance and Facilities** (1,042) follow.
- The most repeated phrases (*not clean*, *not work*, *didn't work*) point to hygiene and equipment faults that guests notice immediately.

## 3. Hotel differences
- Negative share differs widely: some hotels with 30+ reviews have a majority of negative reviews (Norwood Inn and Suites 88.9%, Fiesta Inn and Suites 87.6%).
- Complaints are concentrated: a small group of hotels accounts for a large share of the mentions of each top phrase.

## 4. Recommended priorities
1. **Cleanliness audits** in the hotels where *not clean* is most frequent.
2. **Maintenance checks** for equipment that does not work (air conditioning, television, internet, fixtures).
3. **Room comfort review**: beds, noise, and room condition.
4. **Service follow-up** for hotels with high Staff and Service complaint shares.

## 5. Caveats
- Sentiment is model-predicted. Agreement with star ratings is about 86%, and 771 reviews rated 4–5 stars were predicted negative.
- 1,107 negative reviews have no complaint category, so category counts understate total complaints.
- Some phrases (*non smoking*, *parking lot*) describe topics rather than complaints and need context from the evidence sentences.
- 314 hotels have fewer than 30 reviews, so conclusions about them are tentative.
